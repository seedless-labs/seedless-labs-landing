questions

straight answers.

is seedless a bank?

no. seedless is a money app. bank payouts go through a licensed nigerian payments partner, and your money never sits with us.

who holds my money?

you do. seedless can't move your money. only you can, with your face or fingerprint.

what if i lose my phone?

your sign-in is a passkey, and it's saved to your apple or google account, not only the phone. sign in to that account on your new phone, open seedless, and you're back in. keep that apple or google account safe, because it's your way back.

do i need to know anything about crypto?

no. seedless runs on solana under the hood, but you just see dollars, naira and the people you pay.

can i get paid from deel, upwork or payoneer?

deel: yes. in deel, open withdrawal methods, add stablecoin transfer, pick USDC on the solana network and paste your seedless address from receive. deel charges 2% plus $1. payoneer and upwork: not directly for most accounts yet. if your payoneer account has its stablecoin wallet and lets you send USDC on solana, send a small test amount to your seedless address first. always choose solana as the network. seedless receives USDC on solana today, and more networks are coming.

where can i get it?

seedless is live on google play, the solana dapp store on seeker, and iphone. see download. bank payouts are live in nigeria first.

has seedless been audited?

seedless orchestrates audited infrastructure. seedless itself has not been audited. read our security policy.
