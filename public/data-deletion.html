Data Deletion

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from Seedless, the non-custodial Solana wallet published by Seedless Labs, Inc. and its affiliated operating entity Seedless Labs Limited (together, "Seedless Labs").

Seedless is a self-custody wallet. It has no user accounts, no username or password, and no server that stores your personal data. Your wallet keys are created and held on your own device in hardware-backed secure storage. Because of this, you are always in full control of your data and can delete it yourself at any time.

How to delete your data

All Seedless data lives on your device. To permanently delete it:

  1. Open the Seedless app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete wallet and confirm. This removes your wallet keys and all locally stored wallet data from the device.
  3. To remove everything at once, uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes all Seedless data on the device, including the encrypted key material held in your device's secure storage.

What is deleted

  • Your wallet keys and passkey references (stored only on your device)
  • Local settings, address book entries, and cached balances or transaction history

What cannot be deleted

  • On-chain data. Transactions you have broadcast to the Solana blockchain are part of a public, permanent ledger that Seedless does not own or control. No wallet, including Seedless, can delete on-chain history.

Data retention

Seedless Labs does not operate a server that stores your personal or wallet data, so there is nothing on our side to retain or delete. Wallet address and transaction data are transmitted to third-party Solana infrastructure only in real time to carry out the actions you request, and we keep no copies.
