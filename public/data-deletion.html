Data Deletion

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from Seedless, the money app published by Seedless Labs, Inc. and its affiliated operating entity Seedless Labs Limited.

Data on your phone

Seedless keeps your account data on your phone, in its secure storage.

  1. Sign out: open Seedless, go to Settings and tap Sign out. This removes your active session from the phone. Your passkey is not affected.
  2. Delete everything on the phone: uninstall Seedless. On Android, this deletes all Seedless data on the device, including the keys in its secure storage.
Before you uninstall, move your money out of anything that lives only on this phone. Burner addresses, sends with a link that nobody has collected yet, and your private balance are secured by keys stored only on this device. Once the app is deleted, nobody can recover them, including us.

Your passkey

Your Seedless passkey is saved in your Apple or Google account's password manager. To delete it, open Google Password Manager or iCloud Keychain (Passwords on iPhone), find the Seedless passkey, and delete it. Money held by that account can only be reached with the passkey, so move it out first.

Data we hold

If you have used bank payouts, our payouts service holds your account address, the email or phone number linked to our payments partner, your verification status, and your payout history. To have it deleted, email francis@seedlesslabs.xyz with the subject "Delete my data" and your Seedless account address. We will delete it within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep, and tell you when it is done.

Our payments partner holds its own records of your identity verification and payouts. Tell us in the same email and we will pass your deletion request to the partner.

If you joined our old website waitlist, the same email request deletes that too.

What cannot be deleted

Transactions on the Solana blockchain are public and permanent. No one, including Seedless, can delete on-chain history.
