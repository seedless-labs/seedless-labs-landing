What you can do

Everything your money needs.

Pay any Nigerian bank

Choose a bank, enter the account number, and see who you're paying before you confirm. Type the amount in naira.

Pay with whatever you hold

Short on dollars for a payment? Seedless uses some of your other balance to cover it, and tells you exactly what it used.

Send money to anyone

Send to any account, or share a link with someone who doesn't have Seedless yet. If they never claim it, you get it back.

Hold stocks

Own Tesla, Apple, NVIDIA and more right next to your dollars. When you need naira, sell and send to your bank in one step.

Earn on your dollars

Put your dollars to work and take them out any time. The rate moves with the market.

Questions

The honest answers.

Is Seedless a bank?

No. Seedless is a money app. Bank payouts are handled by a licensed payment partner, and your money never sits with us.

Who holds my money?

You do. Seedless never holds, controls or has access to your funds. Only you can move them, with your face or fingerprint.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. Seedless runs on Solana under the hood, but you just see dollars, naira and the people you pay.

Where can I pay a bank?

Bank payouts are live in Nigeria first. More countries are coming.

Has Seedless been audited?

Seedless orchestrates audited infrastructure. Seedless itself has not been audited. Read our security policy.
