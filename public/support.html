common questions

how do i sign in on a new phone?

your seedless account is protected by a passkey saved in icloud keychain or google password manager. install seedless on the new phone, signed in to the same apple or google account, and choose to sign in with your passkey.

a payment or payout has not arrived

bank payouts usually land within a few minutes. if one has not arrived after 30 minutes, email us with the time of the payout and the amount, and we will trace it.

what does seedless cost?

see fees. sending to another wallet is free.

delete your account

open seedless, go to settings and tap delete account. see data deletion for exactly what is deleted and what stays.
